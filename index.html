Voices under “Recommended” sound the most natural. On a Mac, confirmed: browsers generally don't expose Enhanced/Premium system voices to web pages at all — even ones fully installed and working. Chrome's free network “Google” voices are the most reliable upgrade available to this page.

Sanskrit, Tibetan, and Japanese pronunciation is beyond what a generic system voice can do reliably — the voice speaks the romanized transliteration, which is an approximation, not a pronunciation guide.
